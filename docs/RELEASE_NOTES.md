# Everything Bagel: release notes

## 0.19, the release candidate

This is the version for friends and family to try. It is the whole game. What testers find gets fixed in a small last update, and that update becomes 1.0.

### What is in the game

- **Five stages**, each with its own floor, enemies, hazards, mini-boss, boss and music: the Bakery counter, the Freezer, the Toaster Oven, the Pantry and the Bread Bin.
- **A story** with an intro comic, a line from every boss, and an ending with credits once the Stale King falls.
- **Twelve bagels**, each with its own quirk, one of them a secret.
- **Twelve weapons**, each with an evolution, and two secret recipes that merge two evolved weapons into one.
- **Twelve toppings** and a Bakery with ten upgrades that last between runs.
- **Four ways to play a stage:** a Full Run (about five minutes), a Quick Run (about three), Endless, and Extra Crispy for a harder version of any of them. Practice lets you try builds with nothing at stake.
- **The Daily Run:** one run a day with a twist, the same for everyone, with a streak and a score card to share.
- **Thirty challenges**, a Goals page, a Cookbook of evolutions, weapons and enemies, and a Stats tab.
- **Looks:** hats, trails and sprinkles for your bagel.
- **Made for a phone:** one thumb, portrait, works with no connection, pauses itself when you switch apps and picks the run back up if the phone closes the game.
- **Settings** for music and sound, screen shake, flashing effects, battery saver, left-handed buttons and tips.
- **Your save stays on your phone.** No accounts, no ads, no tracking. A backup code moves your progress to another phone.

### New in 0.19

- A **Skip** button on the level-up screen. It passes on the hand and heals a little.
- A card that shows new players how to **add the game to their home screen**, on iPhone and Android.
- **Copy game info** in Settings, About: a few lines about the version and the phone to paste into a message when something goes wrong. Nothing personal is in it.
- A launch screen, a picture and title for the link when it is sent in a message, and a new front page for this repo.
- Every button takes a thumb, the smallest text is bigger, and every blinking effect follows the Flashing setting.
- A balance pass over every weapon, bagel, stage and mode. The numbers are in [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md).

### Known limits

- The game is made for portrait phones. It plays in a computer window with the arrow keys or WASD, and Space for Blueberry's dash.
- On an iPhone, the game on the home screen keeps its own save, separate from Safari's. Use a backup code to move progress between them.
